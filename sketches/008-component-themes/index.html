What a theme may change and what it never changes

A theme changesSurfaces, borders, text tones, the interface accent (selection, focus, toggles, brand mark) and shadow depth. Ten tokens, nothing else. Corner radius, type scale and density are fixed by the design.
Fixed in every themeThe lime Play button, blue cue markers, deck identity colors A/B/C/D, and the dark waveform glass with its red-to-violet palette. A meaning never changes with the theme.
Honest states in every themeUnavailable controls look disabled and stay disabled. Waveform color is amplitude styling, not frequency analysis. "BPM — analysis pending" replaces the old empty lime box.
