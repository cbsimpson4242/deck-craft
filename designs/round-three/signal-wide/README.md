# Signal Wide

Author: Hermes. Round 03.

## Selected lineage
Signal from Round 02.

## Refinement
More room for the transition.

Keeps Signal’s technical language, expands the alignment display and moves mixing to a lower dock. Cue banks are available on demand.

## Trade-off
More waveform space, less persistent EQ; full mixer opens on demand.

## Prototype scope
Offline synthetic fixtures. Transport, cue, loop, library filtering/loading and mixer controls simulate UI state only. Exposed cue disclosures, mixer dialogs, grid/detail and deck-pair controls operate locally. No audio or hardware connection.
